As AI becomes more integrated into business processes, organizations need more than policies and good intentions. They need **evidence** that AI is being managed responsibly.

**AI Audit Evidence** refers to the records, documents, and information that demonstrate how AI systems are governed, monitored, and controlled.

Examples of AI audit evidence include:

 🔹AI policies and procedures
 🔹Risk and impact assessments
 🔹Training and awareness records
 🔹Testing and validation results
 🔹Approval and governance decisions
 🔹 ncident and monitoring reports

 If an organization uses AI in recruitment, audit evidence may include the impact assessment, testing results, and records showing human review of AI-generated recommendations.

**Key Takeaway:**
Good governance is not just about doing the right thing. It is also about being able to demonstrate it through clear, documented evidence.

**If it isn't documented, it will be difficult to prove it happened.**
