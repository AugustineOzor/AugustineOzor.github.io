Article 6 provides the rules used to classify AI systems as high-risk. Once a system is high-risk, stricter obligations apply to risk, data, documentation, logging, oversight and performance.

## Key Requirements

**Regulated product pathway**: AI used as a safety component, or as a regulated product, may be high-risk when third-party conformity assessment is required.

**Annex III pathway**: Specified sensitive use cases include areas such as biometrics, critical infrastructure, education, employment, essential services, law enforcement, migration and justice.

**Material influence**: The actual intended purpose and influence on decisions matter.

**Limited-task exceptions**: Some narrow procedural or preparatory uses may qualify for an exception when the legal conditions are met.

**Documentation**: Providers should document and justify classification decisions.

## Why Article 6 Matters

Article 6 helps translate the EU AI Act into clearer expectations for accountability, evidence, oversight and responsible operation. The requirement matters because AI governance is credible only when responsibilities and controls are understood and applied in practice.

## Real-World Example

An application tool that only sorts files alphabetically may be limited support. A system that scores candidates and influences shortlisting is much more likely to require high-risk treatment.

## Key Takeaway

**Article 6 is the gateway to the high-risk compliance regime.**
