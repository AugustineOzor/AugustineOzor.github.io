**Meeting recognized standards can make the route to EU AI Act compliance clearer.**

## What Article 40 does

High-risk AI systems or general-purpose AI models that follow relevant harmonised standards may benefit from a presumption of conformity for the requirements those standards cover.

Common standards support consistency, legal certainty and a clearer understanding of what good compliance looks like.

Requirements not covered by a standard must still be demonstrated separately.

## Key takeaway

Harmonised standards do not replace the EU AI Act. They provide a trusted roadmap for meeting covered requirements.
