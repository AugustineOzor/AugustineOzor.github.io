**A compliant AI system should reflect the reality in which it will operate.**

## What Article 42 covers

A high-risk AI system may benefit from a presumption of conformity for relevant data requirements when its training, validation and testing data reflect the intended geographical, behavioral, contextual and functional setting.

Certification under an applicable EU cybersecurity scheme may support a presumption of conformity for the cybersecurity requirements covered by that scheme.

## Important limitation

The presumption applies only to the specific requirements covered. Organizations must still assess and evidence all other applicable obligations.

## Key takeaway

Representative data and recognized assurance can simplify parts of compliance, but they do not replace complete lifecycle governance.
