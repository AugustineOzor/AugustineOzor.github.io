Article 27 asks certain deployers of high-risk AI to assess how use may affect people’s fundamental rights before deployment.

## Key Requirements

**Use context**: Describe how and where the system will operate.

**Affected people**: Identify individuals and groups who may be affected.

**Potential harms**: Assess rights, discrimination, privacy, autonomy and access impacts.

**Oversight and controls**: Define human review and risk mitigation.

**Complaints and remedy**: Provide routes for challenge and concerns.

**Updates**: Review the assessment when relevant facts change.

## Why Article 27 Matters

Article 27 helps translate the EU AI Act into clearer expectations for accountability, evidence, oversight and responsible operation. The requirement matters because AI governance is credible only when responsibilities and controls are understood and applied in practice.

## Real-World Example

A public authority using AI for benefit eligibility assesses error, discrimination, review and complaint risks before the system affects applicants.

## Key Takeaway

**The question is not only “Can we deploy it?” but “Should we deploy it this way?”**
