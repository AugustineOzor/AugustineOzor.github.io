Article 3 is the **foundation of the EU AI Act** because it defines the key terms used throughout the regulation. It explains the roles of organizations involved in AI, how AI systems are classified, the importance of data and biometrics, compliance requirements, and the rules for powerful AI models.

The article can be grouped into five key areas:

🔹 **Part 1: Key AI Players**
 Defines roles such as providers, deployers, importers, and distributors.

🔹 **Part 2: AI Systems and Risk**
 Explains what an AI system is and introduces key risk-related concepts.

🔹 **Part 3: Data and Biometrics**
 Covers training data, biometric data, identification, and verification.

🔹 **Part 4: Compliance and Market Oversight**
 Introduces conformity assessments, CE marking, and regulatory oversight.

🔹 **Part 5: General-Purpose AI and High-Impact Models**
 Defines powerful AI models and addresses potential systemic risks.

**Key Takeaway:**
 *Article 3 creates a common language for AI governance by clearly defining the people, systems, data, and responsibilities covered by the EU AI Act.*
