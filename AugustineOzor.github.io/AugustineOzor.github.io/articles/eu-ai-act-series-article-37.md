Article 37 creates a mechanism to investigate doubts about whether a notified body continues to meet the required standards.

## Key Requirements

**Commission investigation**: The Commission may investigate concerns.

**Authority cooperation**: Notifying authorities provide relevant information.

**Confidentiality**: Sensitive information remains protected.

**Corrective action**: Restrictions, suspension or withdrawal may be required.

**Commission action**: The Commission can act when necessary within the legal process.

**Ongoing competence**: Approval once does not guarantee competence forever.

## Why Article 37 Matters

Article 37 helps translate the EU AI Act into clearer expectations for accountability, evidence, oversight and responsible operation. The requirement matters because AI governance is credible only when responsibilities and controls are understood and applied in practice.

## Real-World Example

If reports suggest a notified body issued certificates without adequate assessment, authorities can investigate and require corrective action.

## Key Takeaway

**Being approved once is not enough. Competence must be maintained.**
