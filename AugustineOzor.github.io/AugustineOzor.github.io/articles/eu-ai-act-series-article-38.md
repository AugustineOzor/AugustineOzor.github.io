**As we move deeper into the EU AI Act, the focus shifts from individual compliance activities to something equally important: consistency.**

## What Article 38 requires

The European Commission must support cooperation and coordination between Notified Bodies involved in high-risk AI conformity assessments.

Sectoral groups help assessment bodies discuss challenges, align approaches and share practical experience.

Notifying authorities must ensure their Notified Bodies participate directly or through representatives.

Knowledge, lessons learned and good practices should be exchanged across the conformity-assessment community.

## Why it matters

Without coordination, similar AI systems could be assessed differently across Member States.

Shared expertise supports more consistent assessments, clearer expectations and stronger confidence in oversight.

## Key takeaway

Article 38 is about collaboration, consistency and continuous learning. Better coordination supports better oversight, and better oversight builds trust in AI.
