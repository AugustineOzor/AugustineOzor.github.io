Article 17 explains how providers should manage compliance through a documented Quality Management System.

## Key Requirements

**Compliance strategy**: Define how obligations are monitored and met.

**Design controls**: Govern development, change and release.

**Testing and validation**: Verify performance before and after deployment.

**Data management**: Control collection, labeling, quality, retention and access.

**Risk management**: Identify, assess, treat and monitor risks.

**Post-market monitoring**: Track real-world performance and issues.

**Incident reporting**: Manage and report serious incidents.

**Records and accountability**: Maintain evidence and clear roles.

## Why Article 17 Matters

Article 17 helps translate the EU AI Act into clearer expectations for accountability, evidence, oversight and responsible operation. The requirement matters because AI governance is credible only when responsibilities and controls are understood and applied in practice.

## Real-World Example

A provider of student-assessment AI needs repeatable processes for data, testing, bias review, oversight, monitoring, incidents and improvement.

## Key Takeaway

**Trustworthy AI requires a strong management system behind the technology.**
