Before discussing AI requirements and obligations, the EU AI Act first defines the key actors involved in the AI ecosystem.

Understanding these roles is essential because responsibilities differ depending on where you fit in the AI value chain.

🔹 **Provider**
 The organization that develops or places an AI system on the market under its name.

🔹 **Deployer**
 The organization or individual that uses the AI system in practice.

🔹 **Importer**
 An entity that brings an AI system from outside the EU into the EU market.

🔹 **Distributor**
 An organization that makes an AI system available within the supply chain.

🔹 **Authorized Representative**
 A representative acting on behalf of a provider based outside the EU.

🔹 **Operator**
 A collective term covering providers, deployers, importers, distributors, and authorized representatives.

**Example:**
 A company develops an AI recruitment tool, while another company uses it to screen job applicants. The developer is the **provider**, and the user organization is the **deployer**.

Understanding these definitions is the first step toward understanding compliance under the EU AI Act.

**AI governance starts with knowing your role and responsibilities.**
