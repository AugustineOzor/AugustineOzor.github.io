# EU AI Act Article 66: Consistency Matters in AI Governance

*By Augustine Ozor*

Imagine complying with the same AI law across several EU countries, but facing very different interpretations in each one.

That could quickly become complicated.

Article 66 of the EU AI Act helps address this by defining the role of the AI Board in supporting consistent and effective implementation across the EU.

The Board helps coordinate national authorities, share best practices and technical expertise, support regulatory sandboxes and real-world testing, advise on general-purpose AI, and encourage common approaches to implementation.

It also connects AI governance with areas such as cybersecurity, data protection, consumer protection and fundamental rights.

The message is clear:

AI governance works better when regulators coordinate rather than operate in isolation.

For organisations, especially those operating across multiple EU Member States, greater consistency can mean clearer expectations and a more predictable approach to compliance.

Good regulation creates the rules.

Good governance helps make those rules work.
