**Compliance should not stop because standards have not caught up.**

## What Article 41 does

When harmonised standards are unavailable, delayed or incomplete, the European Commission may introduce Common Specifications.

These specifications provide a practical route for demonstrating conformity and supporting consistent implementation.

Organizations may use another approach, but they must demonstrate that it achieves an equivalent level of compliance.

## Why it matters

Common Specifications prevent compliance efforts from stalling while formal standards continue to develop.

They also make documentation, ownership and transition planning important because later harmonised standards may require adjustments.

## Key takeaway

No standard available does not mean no path forward. Different path, same compliance objective.
