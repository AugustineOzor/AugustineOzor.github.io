Article 28 focuses on the authorities responsible for assessing, designating and monitoring bodies that evaluate high-risk AI systems.

## Key Requirements

**Assessment**: Evaluate conformity assessment bodies.

**Designation and notification**: Recognize qualified organizations.

**Monitoring**: Continue oversight after designation.

**Independence**: Avoid conflicts and maintain impartiality.

**Separation**: Separate assessment activity from notification decisions.

**Confidentiality and competence**: Protect information and maintain qualified staff.

## Why Article 28 Matters

Article 28 helps translate the EU AI Act into clearer expectations for accountability, evidence, oversight and responsible operation. The requirement matters because AI governance is credible only when responsibilities and controls are understood and applied in practice.

## Real-World Example

Before a certification body can assess high-risk AI, the relevant authority evaluates whether the body is competent and independent.

## Key Takeaway

**The AI assessors must themselves be assessed.**
