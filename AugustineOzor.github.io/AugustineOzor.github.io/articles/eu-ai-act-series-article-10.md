Article 10 rests on a simple principle: AI quality depends heavily on data quality. High-risk AI needs governed training, validation and testing data.

## Key Requirements

**Know the data**: Document origin, collection context and intended use.

**Quality**: Data should be relevant, representative, accurate, complete and suitable.

**Bias**: Identify and mitigate biases that can create discriminatory outcomes.

**Preparation**: Document collection, cleaning, labeling, transformation and updating.

**Context**: Reflect the people and environment in which the system will operate.

**Sensitive data**: Where sensitive data is lawfully used for bias work, strict safeguards apply.

## Why Article 10 Matters

Article 10 helps translate the EU AI Act into clearer expectations for accountability, evidence, oversight and responsible operation. The requirement matters because AI governance is credible only when responsibilities and controls are understood and applied in practice.

## Real-World Example

A recruitment system should use traceable data, assess representation, test for bias and prevent unjustified proxy variables from influencing rankings.

## Key Takeaway

**Garbage in, garbage out is not only a technical problem. It is a governance and compliance risk.**
