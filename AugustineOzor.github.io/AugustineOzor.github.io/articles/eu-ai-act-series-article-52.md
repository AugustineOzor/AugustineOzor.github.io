**Crossing the systemic-risk threshold triggers a formal regulatory process.**

## What Article 52 requires

A provider must notify the European Commission without delay and within the applicable statutory timeframe when a GPAI model meets, or is expected to meet, the relevant threshold.

The provider may submit evidence explaining why the model should not be classified as systemic risk despite meeting the threshold.

The Commission can review and challenge the evidence, and reassessment may be requested where new, objective and detailed grounds arise.

## Why it matters

Self-reporting supports early visibility, but it must be backed by evidence and independent regulatory scrutiny.

## Key takeaway

Powerful models bring a duty to notify, explain and remain accountable.

## Discussion question

*Should providers self-report systemic risk, or should independent assessment play a larger role?*
