**Some general-purpose AI models may be powerful enough to create risks beyond a single organization or use case.**

## How Article 51 works

A GPAI model may be classified as having systemic risk when it demonstrates high-impact capabilities assessed through appropriate technical tools, benchmarks and indicators.

A model trained using compute exceeding 10^25 floating-point operations is presumed to have high-impact capabilities, subject to the Act's process.

The European Commission may also designate a model where its capabilities or impact are equivalent, even if the threshold is not met.

## Why it matters

Systemic-risk identification is only the first step. Providers need clear accountability, effective controls, verification and continuous assurance as capabilities evolve.

## Key takeaway

Greater capability brings greater responsibility.
