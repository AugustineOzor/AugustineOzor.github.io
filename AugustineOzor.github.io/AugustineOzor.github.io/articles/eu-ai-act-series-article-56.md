**Regulation explains what providers must achieve. Codes of Practice can help translate those obligations into practical, measurable actions.**

## What Article 56 does

The AI Office facilitates the development of Union-level Codes of Practice for GPAI obligations, with participation from providers, authorities, industry, academia, civil society, downstream providers and independent experts.

The Codes may address keeping technical information current, training-content summaries, identification and management of systemic risks, documentation, objectives, commitments and appropriate performance indicators.

Participants may report on implementation and outcomes, while the AI Office and the Board monitor whether the Codes achieve their objectives and adequately cover Articles 53 and 55.

Codes can be reviewed and adapted as technology and standards evolve.

## Why it matters

The real value appears when broad legal duties become consistent controls, evidence and measurable operating practices tailored to each provider's models and risks.

## Key takeaway

Principles set the direction. Codes of Practice help make them operational.
