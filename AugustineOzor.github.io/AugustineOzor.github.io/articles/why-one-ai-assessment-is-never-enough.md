As AI adoption grows, many organizations ask:

**"Which assessment should we perform?"**

The answer is simple:

**There is no single assessment that can fully evaluate an AI system.**

Different assessments answer different questions.

### **🔹 AI Impact Assessment**

This focuses on people and outcomes.

It asks:

✅ Who could be affected?

✅ What benefits or harms could result?

✅ What safeguards and human oversight are needed?

### **🔹 Privacy Assessment (DPIA)**

This focuses on data protection.

It asks:

✅ Is personal or sensitive data being used appropriately?

✅ Is there a lawful basis for processing?

✅ Are privacy controls in place?

### **🔹 AI Risk Assessment**

This focuses on risk management.

It asks:

✅ What could go wrong?

✅ How severe would the impact be?

✅ How should the risk be controlled and monitored?

### **🔹 Model Risk Review**

This focuses on the model itself.

It asks:

✅ Is the model reliable?

✅ Is it performing as expected?

✅ Is it fit for its intended purpose?

### **🔹 Security & Adversarial Testing**

This focuses on resilience.

It asks:

✅ Can the system be manipulated?

✅ Is it vulnerable to prompt injection or jailbreaks?

✅ Could it leak sensitive information?

### **🔹 GRC Review**

This focuses on governance and accountability.

It asks:

✅ Were assessments reviewed and approved?

✅ Are controls linked to risks?

✅ Is there audit evidence to support decisions?

### **Why This Matters**

AI systems create risks across people, data, technology, compliance, and business operations.

That's why mature AI governance doesn't rely on a single assessment.

It combines:

✅ Impact reviews
 ✅ Privacy reviews
 ✅ Risk assessments
 ✅ Model validation
 ✅ Security testing
 ✅ Governance oversight

into one repeatable process.

### **Key Takeaway**

**Strong AI governance is not about completing one assessment. It's about bringing multiple perspectives together to build trust, manage risk, and demonstrate accountability.**

The goal isn't just to deploy AI.

The goal is to deploy AI responsibly.
