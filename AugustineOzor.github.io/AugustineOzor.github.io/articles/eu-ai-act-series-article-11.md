Article 11 requires providers to prepare technical documentation before placing a high-risk AI system on the market or putting it into service, and to keep the documentation current.

## Key Requirements

**System description**: Explain the system and intended purpose.

**Development and testing**: Describe design, development, validation and performance.

**Data governance**: Document data sources, preparation and controls.

**Risk management**: Record identified risks and mitigation.

**Compliance evidence**: Show how applicable requirements are met.

**Lifecycle updates**: Update the file as the model, system or technology changes.

## Why Article 11 Matters

Article 11 helps translate the EU AI Act into clearer expectations for accountability, evidence, oversight and responsible operation. The requirement matters because AI governance is credible only when responsibilities and controls are understood and applied in practice.

## Real-World Example

Before launching a high-risk insurance-assessment system, a provider documents the model, data, testing, limitations, risks and controls.

## Key Takeaway

**Technical documentation is a living record, not a one-time file.**
