If Article 11 explains how an AI system was built, Article 12 focuses on how it operates. High-risk systems need appropriate automatic logging capabilities.

## Key Requirements

**Automatic logging**: Record relevant events during operation.

**Traceability**: Support reconstruction of system activity and relevant interactions.

**Risk monitoring**: Help identify incidents, risks and significant changes.

**Post-market monitoring**: Support ongoing oversight after deployment.

**Accountability**: Record relevant human review, intervention or verification.

**Investigation**: Provide evidence for audits, incidents and root-cause analysis.

## Why Article 12 Matters

Article 12 helps translate the EU AI Act into clearer expectations for accountability, evidence, oversight and responsible operation. The requirement matters because AI governance is credible only when responsibilities and controls are understood and applied in practice.

## Real-World Example

If an AI system produces an incorrect identification, logs can help show the model version, event, data source, output and human review.

## Key Takeaway

**No logs. No traceability. No traceability. No accountability.**
