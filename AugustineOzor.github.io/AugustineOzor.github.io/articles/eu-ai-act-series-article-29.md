Article 29 describes the application process for conformity assessment bodies seeking notification.

## Key Requirements

**Application**: Apply to the national notifying authority.

**Assessment scope**: Describe activities, modules and AI system categories.

**Accreditation**: Provide an accreditation certificate where available.

**Alternative evidence**: Otherwise provide documentary evidence of compliance.

**Existing designations**: Include relevant certifications or designations.

**Ongoing currency**: Keep information current and report material changes.

## Why Article 29 Matters

Article 29 helps translate the EU AI Act into clearer expectations for accountability, evidence, oversight and responsible operation. The requirement matters because AI governance is credible only when responsibilities and controls are understood and applied in practice.

## Real-World Example

A medical-device testing laboratory seeking AI notification submits its scope, competence, process and accreditation evidence.

## Key Takeaway

**Before assessing compliance, an organization must prove its own competence.**
