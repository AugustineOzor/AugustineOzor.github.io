Article 18 reminds providers that creating documentation is only half the job. Required records must remain available for the applicable retention period.

## Key Requirements

**Technical documentation**: Retain the Article 11 technical file.

**QMS records**: Retain relevant Article 17 quality records.

**Notified-body documentation**: Keep relevant decisions and approved changes where applicable.

**Declaration of conformity**: Preserve the formal declaration.

**Ten-year period**: Specified records are generally kept for ten years after market placement or putting into service.

**Integrity and access**: Protect records and ensure they can be retrieved.

## Why Article 18 Matters

Article 18 helps translate the EU AI Act into clearer expectations for accountability, evidence, oversight and responsible operation. The requirement matters because AI governance is credible only when responsibilities and controls are understood and applied in practice.

## Real-World Example

A provider launches a high-risk credit system in 2026. Relevant records may need to remain available until at least 2036, subject to the applicable circumstances.

## Key Takeaway

**No documentation means no evidence. Without evidence, compliance becomes difficult to demonstrate.**
