Not all AI uses are acceptable.

**Article 5 **of the **EU AI Act **defines the AI practices that are considered **unacceptable risks** and are therefore prohibited. These include manipulative AI, social scoring, exploitation of vulnerable individuals, certain biometric practices, and specific uses of facial recognition technologies.

The purpose is clear: **protect people's rights, privacy, dignity, and freedoms while promoting trustworthy AI.**

As organizations continue to adopt AI, understanding these boundaries is just as important as understanding the opportunities AI creates.

**Responsible AI starts with knowing what should never be built or deployed.**
