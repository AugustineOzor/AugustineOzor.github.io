As organizations adopt AI, one of the most common questions is:

**What should our AI governance structure look like?**

The answer is simple:

**It depends on the organization.**

AI governance should align with an organization's:

✅ Size
 ✅ Culture
 ✅ Risk profile
 ✅ AI maturity
 ✅ Business objectives
 ✅ Level of AI adoption

Some organizations may start by using existing risk, security, privacy, data, or architecture committees. Others may establish a dedicated AI governance board from the beginning.

### **Key Design Decisions**

🔹 **Decision Rights**

Who can:

- Approve AI use cases?

- Accept residual risk?

- Grant exceptions?

- Stop unsafe deployments?

Clear decision-making authority is essential.

🔹 **Ownership Models**

Every AI system should have clearly defined owners, including:

- Business owners

- Technical owners

- Data owners

- Risk and compliance owners

- Operational owners

Accountability works best when ownership is clear.

🔹 **Review Forums**

Organizations need a place where AI risks, controls, and use cases are reviewed consistently.

This may be an existing governance forum or a dedicated AI governance committee.

🔹 **Escalation Paths**

Not every AI issue should stay at the operational level.

Organizations should define when concerns need to be escalated to:

- Executive leadership

- Legal teams

- Compliance functions

- Board-level oversight

### **Why This Matters**

Many governance models look great in presentations but struggle in practice because they don't reflect how the organization actually operates.

Effective AI governance is not about creating more committees.

It's about creating clear accountability, practical decision-making, and governance processes that people will actually use.

### **Key Takeaway**

**The best AI governance structure is the one that works in your organization.**

A model that aligns with your people, processes, culture, and risk environment will always be more effective than a perfectly designed structure that nobody follows.

**Good AI governance isn't just well-designed. It's usable.**
