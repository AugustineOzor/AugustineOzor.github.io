**When a GPAI model becomes powerful enough to create systemic risk, documentation alone is not enough.**

## What Article 55 requires

Conduct and document model evaluations, including adversarial testing, to identify and mitigate systemic risks.

Assess and address risks that may arise from development, market placement or use at scale.

Track, document and report serious incidents and possible corrective measures to the AI Office and relevant authorities without undue delay.

Maintain an adequate level of cybersecurity protection for the model and its physical infrastructure.

## Why it matters

For systemic-risk models, governance is an ongoing operating responsibility that combines testing, risk mitigation, incident reporting and cybersecurity.

## Key takeaway

More capability means more scrutiny, safeguards and accountability.
