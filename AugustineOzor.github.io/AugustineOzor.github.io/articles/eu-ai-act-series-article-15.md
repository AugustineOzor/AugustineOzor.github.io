A high-risk AI system is trustworthy only if it performs reliably, handles unexpected situations and resists attack. Article 15 brings these expectations together.

## Key Requirements

**Accuracy**: Achieve and document appropriate performance for the intended purpose.

**Robustness**: Continue operating reliably under faults, unusual conditions and unexpected inputs.

**Cybersecurity**: Protect models, data and outputs from manipulation or compromise.

**AI-specific threats**: Consider data poisoning, model poisoning, adversarial inputs, confidentiality attacks and model vulnerabilities.

**Lifecycle monitoring**: Monitor degradation and changing threats.

**Feedback loops**: Address harmful feedback loops where relevant.

## Why Article 15 Matters

Article 15 helps translate the EU AI Act into clearer expectations for accountability, evidence, oversight and responsible operation. The requirement matters because AI governance is credible only when responsibilities and controls are understood and applied in practice.

## Real-World Example

A fraud-detection model should maintain documented performance, handle unusual activity, resist manipulation and avoid harmful feedback loops.

## Key Takeaway

**Trustworthy AI must be accurate, resilient and secure throughout its lifecycle.**
