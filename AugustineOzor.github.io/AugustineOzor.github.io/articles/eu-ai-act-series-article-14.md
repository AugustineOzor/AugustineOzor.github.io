Article 14 requires high-risk AI systems to support meaningful human oversight. The goal is to keep people informed, capable and empowered to intervene.

## Key Requirements

**Understand the system**: Know its capabilities, limitations and expected behavior.

**Monitor performance**: Recognize anomalies and unexpected results.

**Automation bias**: Avoid blindly accepting AI recommendations.

**Interpret outputs**: Assess results before acting.

**Override**: Disregard, change or reverse recommendations where appropriate.

**Stop the system**: Pause or safely interrupt the system when necessary.

## Why Article 14 Matters

Article 14 helps translate the EU AI Act into clearer expectations for accountability, evidence, oversight and responsible operation. The requirement matters because AI governance is credible only when responsibilities and controls are understood and applied in practice.

## Real-World Example

Recruiters should review AI-generated shortlists critically, correct errors, challenge questionable results and retain the final decision.

## Key Takeaway

**AI can support decisions. Accountability must remain human.**
