Article 30 defines the formal process that turns a qualified conformity assessment body into an officially notified body.

## Key Requirements

**Authority review**: The national authority reviews the body.

**EU notification**: The Commission and Member States receive the notification.

**Scope details**: The notification identifies activities, modules, AI system types and competence evidence.

**Objection period**: The applicable review period allows concerns to be raised.

**Resolution**: Concerns are considered before activity begins.

**Transparency**: The process supports consistent EU oversight.

## Why Article 30 Matters

Article 30 helps translate the EU AI Act into clearer expectations for accountability, evidence, oversight and responsible operation. The requirement matters because AI governance is credible only when responsibilities and controls are understood and applied in practice.

## Real-World Example

A body supported by accreditation follows the applicable notification and objection process before officially performing the notified activities.

## Key Takeaway

**Competence gets you considered. Notification makes you official.**
