As AI becomes embedded into business applications and infrastructure, one challenge becomes increasingly clear:

**Embedded AI can hide in plain sight.**

Whether it's an AI feature inside Microsoft 365, Salesforce, Snowflake, or a self-hosted model running in AWS, Azure, or a private cloud, many organizations assume these systems are already governed simply because they sit inside trusted environments.

Unfortunately, that's not always the case.

### **🔹 Data Security & Posture Management (DSPM)**

Before you can protect data, you need to know where it is.

DSPM helps organizations:

✅ Discover sensitive data
 ✅ Classify high-risk information
 ✅ Identify AI-accessible data sources
 ✅ Apply risk-based controls

This is critical because AI may process:

- Customer information

- Financial records

- Health data

- Source code

- Proprietary business information

If data is not classified, organizations cannot effectively manage AI risk.

### **🔹 AI Security & Posture Management (AI-SPM)**

Many AI risks exist because organizations don't know what AI systems are running.

AI-SPM provides visibility into:

✅ AI features enabled in SaaS platforms
 ✅ Self-hosted models and endpoints
 ✅ AI plugins and integrations
 ✅ Configuration and posture risks
 ✅ Ownership and accountability

You can't govern AI that you haven't discovered.

### **🔹 Audit Trail & Data Lineage**

One of the biggest challenges with AI is answering a simple question:

**"How did the AI reach this result?"**

Audit trails and lineage help organizations understand:

✅ Which data was used
 ✅ Which model generated the output
 ✅ Who initiated the request
 ✅ What actions were taken
 ✅ Which policies applied

Without traceability, investigations, audits, compliance reviews, and incident response become much more difficult.

### **Why This Matters**

Embedded AI often feels familiar because it lives inside systems organizations already trust.

But trusted platforms can still introduce:

🔹 Hidden data flows
 🔹 Unapproved AI features
 🔹 Poor visibility into AI decisions
 🔹 Compliance and security risks

### **Key Takeaway**

**Embedded AI should be treated like any other critical enterprise system.**

Whether AI is embedded in SaaS applications or self-hosted in IaaS environments, organizations need:

✅ Data classification
 ✅ AI inventory and posture management
 ✅ Audit trails and lineage

The more AI becomes embedded into everyday business processes, the more important visibility, governance, and accountability become.

**If you can't see it, you can't govern it.**
