Article 26 makes clear that buying a compliant AI system is not enough. Deployers have responsibilities for how high-risk AI is used.

## Key Requirements

**Use as intended**: Follow instructions and approved purpose.

**Human oversight**: Assign competent, trained and authorized people.

**Input data**: Ensure relevant input quality when data is under deployer control.

**Monitoring and incidents**: Monitor operation and act when risks or serious incidents emerge.

**Logs**: Retain logs under deployer control for the applicable period.

**Information**: Inform workers and affected people where required.

**Data protection**: Use provider information to support DPIAs and related obligations.

## Why Article 26 Matters

Article 26 helps translate the EU AI Act into clearer expectations for accountability, evidence, oversight and responsible operation. The requirement matters because AI governance is credible only when responsibilities and controls are understood and applied in practice.

## Real-World Example

A bank using high-risk loan AI assigns trained oversight, governs inputs, monitors outcomes, retains accessible logs and investigates significant issues.

## Key Takeaway

**Providers build the AI. Deployers are responsible for how it is used.**
