AI model assurance starts **before deployment**, not after something goes wrong.

Organizations need structured development practices that make models easier to understand, test, audit, and maintain.

That means:

🔹 **Controlled development**
 Document key design decisions, use approved data sources, maintain version control, and keep development processes reproducible.

🔹 **Robust validation and testing**
 Don't test only for accuracy. Evaluate bias, robustness, security, and real-world behavior. For GenAI, this should also consider hallucinations, prompt injection, unsafe outputs, and data leakage.

🔹 **Ongoing governance**
 Maintain model lineage, approval records, and monitoring for drift, degradation, and unintended behavior.

The principle is simple:

**If you cannot trace it, test it, reproduce it, and monitor it, you will struggle to assure it.**

Unmanaged experimentation may move fast, but it can leave organizations with AI systems that are difficult to trust, audit, or maintain.

**Good AI governance doesn't begin when the model goes live. It begins when the model is being built.**
