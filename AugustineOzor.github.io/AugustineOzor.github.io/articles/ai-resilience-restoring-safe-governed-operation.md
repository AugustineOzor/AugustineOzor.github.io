**AI resilience is more than keeping systems online.**

It is the ability to restore an AI system to a safe, stable, and governed state after something goes wrong.

AI environments can change quickly. Models are updated, prompts are modified, agents gain new capabilities, data sources change, and new tools or integrations are added. Without proper change management, these updates can introduce unexpected risks.

This is why resilient AI operations need:

- Change management to review and approve significant updates
- Version control to track changes to models, prompts, agents, and configurations
- Rollback procedures to return to a known safe version when problems occur
- Clear communication between engineering, security, compliance, operations, and business teams
- Recovery evidence to confirm that the system has been restored safely

When an AI incident occurs, organizations should be able to answer:

What changed? Who approved it? What was affected? Can we roll it back? And can we prove the system is operating safely again?

## Key takeaway

**AI resilience is not simply about getting AI back online. It is about restoring safe, controlled, and governed operation after disruption.**
