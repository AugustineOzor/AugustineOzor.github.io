# EU AI Act Article 58: A Sandbox Still Needs Rules

*By Augustine Ozor*

Article 57 introduces AI Regulatory Sandboxes.

**EU AI Act Article 58 explains how they should work in practice.**

The goal is simple: allow organizations to test innovative AI while ensuring the process is fair, consistent, transparent, and accessible across the EU.

## What Does Article 58 Focus On?

- **Clear access rules:** Eligibility and selection criteria should be transparent and fair.
- **Simple processes:** Application, participation, monitoring, exit, and reporting procedures should be clearly defined.
- **Support for SMEs and start-ups:** Access should be free for SMEs, including start-ups, with authorities able to recover exceptional costs fairly where applicable.
- **Regulatory learning:** Sandboxes should help both organizations and regulators learn from testing, risks, controls, and real-world implementation.
- **Consistency across the EU:** Common rules help reduce fragmentation between Member States while still allowing flexibility in how sandboxes operate.

A regulatory sandbox only works if organizations can actually access and use it.

Article 58 helps ensure that sandboxes do not become complicated regulatory exercises available only to large companies with significant resources.

Article 58 is about making responsible AI experimentation practical and accessible.

Innovation needs flexibility.

Regulation needs consistency.

Good sandbox design needs both.

> **Update (Digital Omnibus on AI, in force since July 2026):** National authorities now have until 2 August 2027 to establish at least one AI regulatory sandbox, and the AI Office may also create a sandbox at EU level. Check the consolidated Regulation for the current wording.
