Article 23 makes importers an active checkpoint for high-risk AI systems entering the EU market.

## Key Requirements

**Verify conformity assessment**: Confirm the provider completed the required process.

**Check documentation**: Ensure technical documentation is available.

**CE marking and declaration**: Verify the required marking and declaration.

**Authorized representative**: Confirm appointment where required.

**Stop non-compliant market activity**: Do not place a system on the market when there is reason to believe it is non-compliant.

**Records and cooperation**: Keep required records and support authorities.

**Storage and transport**: Do not compromise compliance conditions.

## Why Article 23 Matters

Article 23 helps translate the EU AI Act into clearer expectations for accountability, evidence, oversight and responsible operation. The requirement matters because AI governance is credible only when responsibilities and controls are understood and applied in practice.

## Real-World Example

An importer of a high-risk recruitment system checks conformity evidence, documentation, marking, declaration and representative details before market placement.

## Key Takeaway

**Trust, but verify. Compliance responsibility does not stop at the border.**
