Article 22 addresses authorized representatives for certain providers established outside the EU who offer high-risk AI systems in the Union.

## Key Requirements

**Written mandate**: The representative operates under documented authority.

**Verification**: Check that required conformity and documentation activities have been addressed.

**Records**: Keep specified records available to authorities.

**Regulatory requests**: Provide information and cooperate with authorities.

**Registration and mitigation**: Support relevant registration and risk-reduction activities.

**Non-compliance**: Terminate the mandate and inform authorities when required if the provider breaches obligations.

## Why Article 22 Matters

Article 22 helps translate the EU AI Act into clearer expectations for accountability, evidence, oversight and responsible operation. The requirement matters because AI governance is credible only when responsibilities and controls are understood and applied in practice.

## Real-World Example

A non-EU provider offering high-risk medical AI in France and Germany appoints an EU-based representative before market access.

## Key Takeaway

**The authorized representative is a key EU accountability and regulatory-contact point.**
