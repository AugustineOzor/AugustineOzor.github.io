**Building a powerful model is no longer enough. Providers must be able to document it, explain it and support those who use it.**

## What Article 53 requires

Maintain technical documentation covering the model's development, training, testing and evaluation.

Provide relevant information and documentation to downstream providers integrating the model into their systems.

Maintain a policy for compliance with EU copyright law.

Publish a sufficiently detailed summary of the content used to train the model, using the required template.

## Open-source point

Certain open-source GPAI models may benefit from limited exemptions, but the position changes where a model presents systemic risk.

## Key takeaway

Trust in GPAI begins with documentation, transparency, copyright compliance and support across the value chain.

## Discussion question

*Which obligation will be hardest: documentation, training-data transparency, copyright compliance or downstream support?*
