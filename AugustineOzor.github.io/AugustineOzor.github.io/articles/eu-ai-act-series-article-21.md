Article 21 requires providers of high-risk AI systems to cooperate with competent authorities after a reasoned request.

## Key Requirements

**Documentation**: Provide information needed to demonstrate compliance.

**Logs**: Provide access to relevant logs under the provider’s control.

**Appropriate language**: Respond in a requested language that authorities can understand.

**Cooperation**: Support reasonable regulatory requests.

**Confidentiality**: Trade secrets and confidential information remain protected under applicable rules.

**Readiness**: Maintain organized evidence and accountable contacts.

## Why Article 21 Matters

Article 21 helps translate the EU AI Act into clearer expectations for accountability, evidence, oversight and responsible operation. The requirement matters because AI governance is credible only when responsibilities and controls are understood and applied in practice.

## Real-World Example

A regulator investigating a complaint may request the technical file, risk evidence, governance records and relevant available logs.

## Key Takeaway

**Compliance is not only what you do. It is what you can prove.**
