As organizations continue to adopt AI, it is important to understand that **learning AI governance** and **assessing AI readiness** are two different, but complementary, activities.

📚 **AI Governance Learning** focuses on building knowledge and understanding. The course is centered around five key AI Governance Pillars:

• Governance & Leadership
 • Risk, Impact & Compliance
 • Data & Model Lifecycle
 • Secure Operations & Resilience
 • Ethics, Transparency & Acceptable Use

These pillars help learners understand how AI should be governed, managed, and used responsibly within an organization.

🔍 **AI Readiness Assessment**, on the other hand, focuses on validation. It evaluates whether the necessary controls are actually implemented and supported by evidence across a broader AI security and operational landscape, including:

• AI Governance & Responsible AI
 • Data for AI Systems
 • Infrastructure & Network Security
 • Model, Agent & Deployment Security
 • Communication Interfaces Security
 • AI Application Security
 • Identity & Access Management
 • Threat Detection & Incident Response

### **The Key Takeaway**

Think of it this way:

✅ **The course teaches the governance model.**
 ✅ **The assessment verifies that controls are implemented and operating effectively.**

Learning provides the foundation, while assessment demonstrates readiness. Organizations need both to build AI systems that are secure, trustworthy, compliant, and responsible.

As AI adoption accelerates, moving from *understanding governance* to *evidencing implementation* will be essential for achieving true AI readiness.
