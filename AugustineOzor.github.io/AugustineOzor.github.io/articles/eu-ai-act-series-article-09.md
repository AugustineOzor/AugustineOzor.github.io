Article 9 requires providers of high-risk AI systems to establish and maintain a continuous risk-management system throughout the AI lifecycle.

## Key Requirements

**Identify risks**: Identify known and reasonably foreseeable risks to health, safety and fundamental rights.

**Assess risks**: Evaluate risks from intended use, reasonably foreseeable misuse and changing conditions.

**Implement controls**: Use proportionate measures to reduce risk.

**Test**: Test during development and before deployment, and update testing when needed.

**Monitor and improve**: Use operational evidence, incidents and feedback to update controls.

**Vulnerable groups**: Consider impacts on children and other vulnerable groups where relevant.

## Why Article 9 Matters

Article 9 helps translate the EU AI Act into clearer expectations for accountability, evidence, oversight and responsible operation. The requirement matters because AI governance is credible only when responsibilities and controls are understood and applied in practice.

## Real-World Example

A bank using AI for loan-support decisions tests for bias, reviews complaints, monitors outcomes and adjusts controls as new risks emerge.

## Key Takeaway

**Trustworthy AI requires continuous risk management, not one-time compliance.**
