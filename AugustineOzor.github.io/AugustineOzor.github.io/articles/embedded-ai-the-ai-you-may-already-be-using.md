When people think about AI risk, they often think about tools like ChatGPT or Claude.

But some of the most impactful AI in organizations isn't on a separate website at all.

It's already embedded inside the applications employees use every day.

Think:

☁️ Microsoft 365 Copilot
 ☁️ Salesforce Einstein
 ☁️ Snowflake Cortex
 ☁️ Other AI-powered SaaS features

In many cases, these capabilities can be enabled with a license upgrade or a simple admin setting. That means AI may already be active in production systems without a dedicated AI project or formal governance review.

### **The Hidden Challenge**

Because embedded AI lives inside trusted platforms, it can appear safe by default.

However, organizations should still ask three important questions:

🔹 **Can we see where our data goes?**
 Often only partially. Data routing, retention, and model interactions may occur behind the scenes with limited visibility.

🔹 **Can we control who uses AI features?**
 Sometimes. Access controls vary significantly between vendors, and AI capabilities may not always align with existing RBAC or governance policies.

🔹 **Can we audit what the AI saw or produced?**
 Often only to a limited degree. Many SaaS platforms do not yet provide detailed prompt, completion, or data lineage records.

### **Why This Matters**

Embedded AI can introduce new data flows, new processing activities, and new risks, even when operating inside systems that were previously approved.

As a result, AI features should not be treated as just another product enhancement.

They should be treated as **new AI assets** that deserve:

✅ Data flow reviews
 ✅ Access control reviews
 ✅ Vendor due diligence
 ✅ Logging and monitoring assessments
 ✅ Governance oversight

### **Key Takeaway**

**Embedded AI can hide in plain sight.**

Just because AI is built into a trusted platform doesn't mean its risks are automatically understood or managed.

The most effective organizations are treating Embedded AI with the same level of governance attention they apply to Public AI and Homegrown AI solutions.

**Trusted platform ≠ automatically trusted AI.**
