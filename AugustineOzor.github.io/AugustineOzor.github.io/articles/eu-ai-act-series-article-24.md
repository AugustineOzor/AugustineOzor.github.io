Article 24 makes distributors a compliance checkpoint before high-risk AI reaches customers.

## Key Requirements

**Verify before distribution**: Check marking, declaration, instructions and relevant provider/importer details.

**Stop distribution**: Do not distribute when there is reason to believe the system is non-compliant.

**Storage and transport**: Protect compliance conditions.

**Corrective action**: Support correction, withdrawal or recall where appropriate.

**Notification**: Inform the provider, importer and authorities where required.

**Cooperation**: Provide information and support investigations.

## Why Article 24 Matters

Article 24 helps translate the EU AI Act into clearer expectations for accountability, evidence, oversight and responsible operation. The requirement matters because AI governance is credible only when responsibilities and controls are understood and applied in practice.

## Real-World Example

A distributor preparing to sell a recruitment AI verifies the required information and takes action if later evidence suggests harmful or non-compliant behavior.

## Key Takeaway

**Verify before distribution. Act when risks emerge.**
