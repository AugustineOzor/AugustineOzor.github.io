**A CE mark is not the final step. For certain high-risk AI systems, Article 49 also requires registration.**

## What Article 49 does

Providers and, in specified cases, deployers must register relevant high-risk AI systems in the EU database before placing them on the market or putting them into service.

Registration captures key information about the system, its provider, intended purpose and conformity status.

## Important distinction

Registration is one obligation, not the finish line. Risk management, documentation, logging, human oversight, cybersecurity and post-market monitoring continue throughout the lifecycle.

## Enterprise lesson

Internal AI inventories apply the same principle: organizations cannot govern what they cannot identify, assign and track.

## Key takeaway

Registration provides visibility. Governance provides authority. Architecture and controls must enforce approved behavior at runtime.
