**Building an AI model outside the EU does not place a provider outside the reach of the EU AI Act.**

## What Article 54 requires

Before placing a GPAI model on the EU market, a provider established outside the EU must appoint an authorized representative established in the Union, subject to the Act's conditions and exceptions.

The appointment must be supported by a written mandate and clear responsibilities.

The representative supports regulatory contact, verifies that required documentation exists, keeps relevant records available and cooperates with the AI Office and competent authorities.

## Why it matters

Appointing a representative is only the starting point. Effective cross-border accountability depends on documentation, information, cooperation and the ability to act when authorities engage.

## Key takeaway

AI may be global, but regulatory responsibility must remain clear and accessible.
