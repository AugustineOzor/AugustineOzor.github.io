Article 16 shifts attention from what high-risk AI must do to who is responsible for making it happen: the provider.

## Key Requirements

**Meet high-risk requirements**: Ensure continuing compliance.

**Technical documentation**: Maintain accurate and current evidence.

**Quality management**: Operate a structured QMS.

**Logs and records**: Support traceability and monitoring.

**Conformity assessment**: Complete the required assessment before market activity.

**Declaration and CE marking**: Issue the declaration and apply marking where required.

**Registration**: Complete applicable registration.

**Corrective action**: Act when issues emerge and cooperate with authorities.

## Why Article 16 Matters

Article 16 helps translate the EU AI Act into clearer expectations for accountability, evidence, oversight and responsible operation. The requirement matters because AI governance is credible only when responsibilities and controls are understood and applied in practice.

## Real-World Example

A provider of creditworthiness AI must complete conformity activities, maintain evidence and controls, register where required and respond to problems.

## Key Takeaway

**If you are the provider, compliance is your responsibility from design through deployment and beyond.**
