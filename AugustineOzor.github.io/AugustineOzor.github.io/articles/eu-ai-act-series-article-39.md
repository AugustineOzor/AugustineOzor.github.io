**AI regulation may be European, but AI compliance is increasingly global.**

## What Article 39 says

A conformity assessment body established outside the EU may participate only where an agreement exists between the EU and the relevant third country.

The body must meet the applicable EU requirements or demonstrate an equivalent level of compliance.

## Why it matters

Article 39 creates a route for qualified non-EU organizations to support conformity assessments without lowering expectations for competence, independence or trust.

## Key takeaway

Same standards. Same trust. Different location.
