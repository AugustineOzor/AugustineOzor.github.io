**What document formally says, 'We take responsibility for this AI system'? Article 47 points to the EU Declaration of Conformity.**

## What providers must do

Prepare a declaration that clearly identifies the high-risk AI system and states that applicable requirements have been met.

Keep the declaration accurate and up to date, and retain it for the required period.

Where multiple EU laws require declarations, a single declaration may cover all applicable obligations.

## Why it matters

The declaration is meaningful only when the evidence behind the signature can withstand scrutiny. It connects technical evidence with formal ownership.

## Key takeaway

Compliance is demonstrated through evidence. Accountability is demonstrated through ownership.

## Discussion question

*Does the declaration strengthen accountability, or risk becoming another document in the compliance folder?*
