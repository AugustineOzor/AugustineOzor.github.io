As AI continues to transform industries, the EU AI Act provides a common set of rules to ensure AI is developed and used responsibly across Europe. Article 1 explains the purpose of the regulation and what it covers. The goal is simple: encourage AI innovation while protecting people, their rights, safety and privacy.

## Key Requirements

**AI systems in the EU**: Sets common rules for how AI systems can be developed, sold and used across EU Member States.

**Prohibited AI practices**: Bans certain AI uses that could pose unacceptable risks to people’s rights and freedoms.

**High-risk AI systems**: Introduces stricter requirements for systems used in sensitive areas such as healthcare, education, employment and financial services.

**Transparency requirements**: Ensures people know when they are interacting with AI or when AI is involved in important processes.

**General-purpose AI models**: Introduces rules for powerful models, including large language models.

**Monitoring and enforcement**: Creates oversight and enforcement mechanisms.

**Support for innovation**: Supports innovation, including for SMEs and start-ups, while maintaining responsible practices.

## Why Article 1 Matters

Article 1 helps translate the EU AI Act into clearer expectations for accountability, evidence, oversight and responsible operation. The requirement matters because AI governance is credible only when responsibilities and controls are understood and applied in practice.

## Real-World Example

A company uses AI to screen job applications. Article 1 establishes the foundation for rules intended to make that process fair, transparent and respectful of candidates. A hospital using AI to support medical decisions must also consider safety, reliability and human oversight.

## Key Takeaway

**Article 1 is the “why” behind the EU AI Act. One rulebook. One approach. Trustworthy AI for everyone.**
