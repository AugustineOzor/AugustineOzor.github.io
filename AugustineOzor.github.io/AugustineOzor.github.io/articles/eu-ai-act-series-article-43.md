**Not all high-risk AI systems take the same road to compliance.**

## The possible routes

Many high-risk AI systems may use an internal-control conformity assessment under Annex VI.

Some situations require Notified Body involvement under Annex VII, including where relevant standards are unavailable, incomplete or not applied as required.

AI embedded in products covered by sector-specific EU legislation follows the applicable product-assessment route while meeting relevant AI Act obligations.

## Lifecycle consideration

A substantial modification may trigger a new conformity assessment. For systems that evolve over time, conformity assessment should be planned as a lifecycle activity.

## Key takeaway

Different systems may follow different assessment routes, but the goal remains safety, accountability and regulatory compliance.
