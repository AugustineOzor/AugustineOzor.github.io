**Getting certified is not the hard part. Staying compliant is.**

## What Article 44 covers

Certificates issued through conformity assessment have defined validity periods and may be renewed following reassessment.

A Notified Body may restrict, suspend or withdraw a certificate where the system no longer meets applicable requirements.

Providers may be required to take corrective action, and decisions must be reasoned and open to appeal through the applicable process.

## Why it matters

Models, data, performance, intended use and risks change. Ongoing governance must identify what changed, assess whether conformity is affected and preserve the supporting evidence.

## Key takeaway

Certification demonstrates conformity at a point in time. Continuous monitoring and governance help maintain it.

## Discussion question

*What is harder: achieving initial AI compliance or maintaining it as systems evolve?*
