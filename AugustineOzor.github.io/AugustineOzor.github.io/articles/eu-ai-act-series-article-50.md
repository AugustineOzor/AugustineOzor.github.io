**If people cannot tell that AI is involved, is the interaction truly transparent?**

## What Article 50 covers

People should be informed when they are interacting directly with an AI system unless this is obvious from the context.

Providers of systems that generate synthetic content must support identification of AI-generated or manipulated outputs.

Affected people must receive appropriate notice when emotion-recognition or biometric-categorization systems are used, subject to applicable exceptions.

Deepfakes and certain AI-generated public-interest content require disclosure, subject to the conditions and exceptions in the Act.

## Why it matters

Transparency is not just a label. People need enough information to understand when AI is shaping an interaction or piece of content.

## Key takeaway

No hidden AI. Clear disclosure. Meaningful transparency supports informed judgment and digital trust.
