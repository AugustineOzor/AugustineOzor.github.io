Article 34 focuses on how notified bodies should carry out conformity assessment activities.

## Key Requirements

**Proper assessment**: Use the required conformity assessment procedures.

**Proportionality**: Consider provider size, structure, sector and system complexity.

**SMEs and start-ups**: Avoid unnecessary administrative burden where possible.

**Required protection**: Proportionality must not reduce regulatory rigor or protection.

**Authority support**: Provide relevant documentation to the notifying authority.

**Consistency**: Apply controlled methods and evidence standards.

## Why Article 34 Matters

Article 34 helps translate the EU AI Act into clearer expectations for accountability, evidence, oversight and responsible operation. The requirement matters because AI governance is credible only when responsibilities and controls are understood and applied in practice.

## Real-World Example

A start-up developing high-risk healthcare AI may receive a proportionate assessment process, but it still needs to meet the same substantive regulatory requirements.

## Key Takeaway

**Proportionate does not mean weaker. It means applying rigor in the right way.**
