**Before an AI system goes live, one simple question matters: Is it truly ready for production?**

An AI Release Checklist provides a final governance gate before deployment.

Technical performance alone is not enough. An AI system may produce good results but still have gaps in security, privacy, compliance, documentation, monitoring, or accountability.

A practical release checklist should confirm:

- Model validation and performance testing are complete
- Bias and fairness risks have been assessed
- Security and privacy reviews have been completed
- Compliance requirements have been checked
- Required documentation and approvals are in place
- Human oversight is clearly defined where needed
- Monitoring is ready for post-deployment risks
- Rollback procedures exist if something goes wrong
- Residual risks have been identified and formally accepted

Without a consistent release process, organizations may deploy AI systems with unresolved vulnerabilities, missing controls, policy violations, or unclear ownership.

An AI release checklist does not need to be complicated. Its value comes from creating consistency, accountability, and evidence that important checks were completed before deployment.

## Key takeaway

**AI production readiness is not just about whether the model works. It is about whether the AI system is safe, secure, compliant, governed, and ready to operate responsibly.**
