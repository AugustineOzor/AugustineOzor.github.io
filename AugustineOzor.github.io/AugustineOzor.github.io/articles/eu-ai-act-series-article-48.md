**What do the letters CE mean for a high-risk AI system? Article 48 makes conformity visible.**

## What Article 48 requires

The CE marking must be visible, legible and accessible.

For digital AI systems, the marking may be displayed digitally through the interface or other electronic means.

Where a Notified Body participated in the assessment, its identification number accompanies the CE marking.

The same marking may support conformity under multiple applicable EU laws.

## Key takeaway

The conformity assessment happens behind the scenes. The CE marking is the visible signal that the applicable process has been completed.
