Employees are already using AI tools like **ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Copilot**.

The real governance challenge isn't stopping them.

**It's helping them use AI safely and responsibly.**

That's where **BYOAI (Bring Your Own AI)** governance comes in.

A strong BYOAI program should focus on three things:

🔹 **OWASP Top 10 for LLMs**

- **Prompt Injection (LLM01):** Establish guardrails for using public AI tools.

- **Excessive Agency (LLM06):** Ensure users understand the limitations of AI, especially agent capabilities.

- **Sensitive Information Disclosure (LLM02):** Train employees on what data can and cannot be pasted or uploaded.

🔹 **NIST AI RMF - Govern Function**

- Define roles and responsibilities.

- Establish acceptable-use guidance.

- Create accountability for AI use.

🔹 **ISO/IEC 42001 - Clause 5.3**

- Assign responsibility for AI oversight.

- Ensure usage is evaluated and documented.

- Support ongoing governance and accountability.

### **What Does This Look Like in Practice?**

✅ A GenAI Acceptable Use Policy

✅ A short list of approved AI tools

✅ Corporate AI instances with appropriate controls

✅ Clear user guidance and training

One lesson I've seen repeatedly:

**When people have a safe and approved way to use AI, shadow AI usage tends to decrease.**

Governance works best when it enables responsible innovation rather than simply saying "no."

### **Key Takeaway**

**BYOAI governance is about providing a safe "yes," not enforcing a blanket "no."**

The goal is to help employees use AI productively while protecting the organization's data, reputation, and compliance obligations.
