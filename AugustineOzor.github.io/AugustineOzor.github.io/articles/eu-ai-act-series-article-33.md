Article 33 allows notified bodies to use subsidiaries or subcontractors, but accountability remains with the notified body.

## Key Requirements

**Equivalent standards**: The subcontractor or subsidiary must meet applicable requirements.

**Authority visibility**: Relevant use must be disclosed to the notifying authority as required.

**Provider agreement**: The provider’s agreement is required for subcontracting activities where specified.

**Responsibility**: The notified body remains accountable for the assessment.

**Records**: Qualification and activity records must be retained for the applicable period.

**Oversight**: Delegated work must be reviewed and controlled.

## Why Article 33 Matters

Article 33 helps translate the EU AI Act into clearer expectations for accountability, evidence, oversight and responsible operation. The requirement matters because AI governance is credible only when responsibilities and controls are understood and applied in practice.

## Real-World Example

A notified body uses a specialist medical-AI firm for part of an assessment. The specialist must be qualified, but the notified body remains accountable for the result.

## Key Takeaway

**You can delegate the work. You cannot outsource accountability.**
