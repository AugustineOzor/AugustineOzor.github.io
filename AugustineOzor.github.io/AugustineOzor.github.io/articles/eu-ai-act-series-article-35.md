Article 35 supports transparency by assigning identification numbers and maintaining public information about notified bodies.

## Key Requirements

**Unique identification**: Each notified body receives an official identification number.

**One identifier**: The same body generally retains one number across relevant EU notifications.

**Public listing**: The Commission maintains an accessible list.

**Authorized scope**: The list shows relevant notified activities and status.

**Provider due diligence**: Providers can verify assessors before engagement.

**Trust**: Common verified information strengthens confidence.

## Why Article 35 Matters

Article 35 helps translate the EU AI Act into clearer expectations for accountability, evidence, oversight and responsible operation. The requirement matters because AI governance is credible only when responsibilities and controls are understood and applied in practice.

## Real-World Example

A healthcare AI provider checks the official register to confirm that an assessor is notified for the required activity before signing a contract.

## Key Takeaway

**One number. One list. Publicly verified.**
