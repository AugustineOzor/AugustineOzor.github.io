**Can a high-risk AI system be used before completing conformity assessment? In limited circumstances, Article 46 provides an exception.**

## When it may apply

A market-surveillance authority may temporarily authorize certain high-risk AI systems for exceptional reasons of public security, protection of life and health, environmental protection, critical infrastructure or another important public interest.

The full conformity assessment must still be completed, and authorization may be withdrawn if the system does not meet requirements.

## Planning for refusal

If authorization is refused, use must stop and affected outputs may need to be discarded. Organizations therefore need traceability to identify where outputs influenced decisions and determine what requires human review.

## Key takeaway

Emergency use is possible, but it is not a shortcut around accountability. Governance, logging and exit planning matter from the start.

## Discussion question

*Does temporary authorization strike the right balance between urgency and public safety?*
