Article 20 requires providers to act when a high-risk AI system is non-compliant or presents relevant risk.

## Key Requirements

**Immediate correction**: Bring the system into conformity.

**Withdrawal, disablement or recall**: Use stronger market action where necessary.

**Prompt investigation**: Investigate risks without undue delay.

**Inform value-chain actors**: Notify deployers, importers, distributors and representatives as relevant.

**Authorities and notified bodies**: Notify the appropriate bodies where required.

**Evidence**: Document root cause, action, retesting and closure.

## Why Article 20 Matters

Article 20 helps translate the EU AI Act into clearer expectations for accountability, evidence, oversight and responsible operation. The requirement matters because AI governance is credible only when responsibilities and controls are understood and applied in practice.

## Real-World Example

If recruitment AI disadvantages qualified candidates, the provider should contain the issue, investigate, correct or disable the function, notify appropriate parties and validate the fix.

## Key Takeaway

**Fix it. Inform the right people. Demonstrate accountability.**
