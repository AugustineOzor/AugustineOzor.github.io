Many AI governance challenges are not caused by technology.

They happen because the right people were not involved at the right time.

AI governance is a team effort. It requires ongoing communication with everyone who owns, uses, approves, monitors, depends on, or is affected by an AI system.

### **Start with the Right Stakeholders**

Before deploying AI, ask:

✅ Who owns the system?

✅ Who uses it?

✅ Who approves it?

✅ Who monitors it?

✅ Who could be affected by its decisions?

Missing a stakeholder can mean missing a critical risk.

### **Communication Is Just as Important**

Stakeholders need to understand:

🔹 Why the AI is being used

🔹 What its limitations are

🔹 What risks are being managed

🔹 What decisions have been made

🔹 How incidents and concerns will be handled

Transparency builds trust and accountability.

### **Don't Forget Feedback**

Some of the most valuable AI governance insights come from feedback.

Business users may spot operational issues.

Security teams may identify misuse patterns.

Legal and compliance teams may identify regulatory concerns.

Customers and employees may raise questions about fairness, transparency, or trust.

Listening to stakeholders helps organizations improve AI governance continuously.

### **Key Takeaway**

**Good AI governance is not just about policies and controls. It's about people, communication, and feedback.**

The organizations that engage stakeholders effectively are often the ones that manage AI risks most successfully.
