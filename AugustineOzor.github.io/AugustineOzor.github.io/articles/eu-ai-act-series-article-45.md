**What prevents a provider from simply seeking a more favorable assessment after a refusal? Information sharing.**

## What Article 45 requires

Notified Bodies must communicate relevant information to notifying authorities and other Notified Bodies.

This includes information relating to certificates that are issued, refused, restricted, suspended or withdrawn, together with relevant conformity-assessment activities.

Confidential information must still be handled responsibly.

## Why it matters

Information sharing supports consistency, reduces fragmented oversight and helps assessment bodies make informed decisions.

## Key takeaway

Article 45 balances cooperation with confidentiality. Better information flows support better oversight.
