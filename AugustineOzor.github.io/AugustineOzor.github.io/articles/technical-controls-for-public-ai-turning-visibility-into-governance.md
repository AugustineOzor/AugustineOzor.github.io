Public AI tools such as **ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and public Copilot experiences** are becoming part of everyday work.

While these tools can significantly improve productivity, they also introduce risks around data sharing, access management, and visibility.

The challenge is simple:

**You may not own the AI platform, but you still need to govern how it's used.**

### **🔹 AI Gateway: Control What Goes In and Out**

An AI Gateway acts as a checkpoint between users and public AI services.

It helps organizations:

✅ Inspect prompts and responses
 ✅ Detect sensitive information
 ✅ Apply policy controls
 ✅ Log AI activity
 ✅ Reduce data leakage risks

Without visibility into what is being shared, organizations may not realize sensitive business information is leaving the environment.

### **🔹 Identity & Access Management (IAM)**

Not everyone needs the same level of access to AI tools.

IAM helps organizations:

** 🔹** Enforce Single Sign-On (SSO)
 **🔹** Control who can access approved AI services
 **🔹** Restrict personal account usage where appropriate
 **🔹** Apply role-based access controls (RBAC)
 **🔹** Manage plugin and integration permissions

Strong identity controls help ensure AI access is aligned with business needs and security requirements.

### **🔹 AI Security & Posture Management (AISPM)**

You can't govern what you can't see.

AISPM helps organizations:

**🔹** Inventory AI tools in use
 **🔹** Identify shadow AI
 **🔹** Monitor plugins and integrations
 **🔹** Track approved and unapproved AI services
 **🔹** Apply governance policies consistently

Many organizations discover that employees are using far more AI tools than security teams realize.

### **Why This Matters**

Public AI may exist outside your infrastructure, but it shouldn't exist outside your governance.

The goal is not to block AI adoption.

The goal is to create safeguards that allow employees to use AI productively while reducing risks related to:

🔹 Data leakage
 🔹 Unapproved integrations
 🔹 Shadow AI
 🔹 Compliance violations
 🔹 Lack of visibility

### **Key Takeaway**

**Public AI should be easy to use, but not invisible.**

By combining **AI Gateways, IAM, and AISPM**, organizations can improve visibility, strengthen controls, and support safer AI adoption at scale.
